Photography credits

The industrial photography on this site is real, licensed photography — not stock mock-ups or generated imagery.

Every image is sourced from Wikimedia Commons under CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA licences, and is self-hosted rather than hot-linked. CC BY and CC BY-SA require attribution, which is given in full below.

None of these photographs depicts {{ site.name }}, its premises, its personnel or its own consignments. They illustrate the categories of equipment, materials and operations described on each page. Replace them with the company's own photography when it is available.

The complete machine-readable record, including each file's source URL, author and licence, is at public/assets/images/attribution.json in the project, with a formatted table in docs/IMAGE_ATTRIBUTION.md.

The per-file credit table is maintained in docs/IMAGE_ATTRIBUTION.md and attribution.json. A build step can render it here once the company confirms whether it intends to keep this photography or substitute its own.
