The work behind the list

Different clients call on different parts of the same chain.

  • Project supply

    Material packages phased to a construction or installation programme, consolidated to reduce the number of shipments and interfaces.

  • Maintenance and shutdown

    Spares, consumables and rigging staged against a planned outage date, with critical items identified and held ahead of demand.

  • Breakdown response

    Urgent sourcing and expedited movement when production has already stopped and the routing decision is driven by downtime cost.

  • Freight and clearance only

    Forwarding, documentation and customs clearance for goods a client has sourced themselves.
