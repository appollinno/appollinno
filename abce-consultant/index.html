Quality and safety are the specification, not the sales pitch

In high-criticality service, an item that is nearly right is a hazard. We source against the standard that governs the application, collect the material and conformity documentation with the goods, and verify the consignment against the purchase order before it is accepted.

Standards we work to

  • API Spec 5L, 6A and 6D
  • ASME pressure equipment, boilers, piping and flanges
  • ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001
  • ASTM and EN civil, structural and fastener standards

A distinction we keep clear. The standards above are what we work to and source products against. They are not a claim that {{ site.name }} itself holds certification to any of them. Where you need evidence of a certification — ours or a manufacturer's — ask us and we will tell you exactly what exists.
