What to send us with an enquiry

The more of this you have, the faster and more accurate the quotation. If you only have some of it, send what you have — establishing the rest is part of the job.

Enquiry checklist
Item and quantityWhat the item is and how many, with units.
Specification or standardThe governing standard, grade, class or OEM part number if known.
Service conditionsMedium, pressure, temperature and, for electrical equipment, area classification.
Documentation requiredMaterial certificates, test reports or conformity documents your QA department needs.
Delivery locationPort, site or warehouse, and whether inland transport is required.
Required on site dateThe date the material has to be usable, not the date you would like it shipped.
