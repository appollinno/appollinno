What happens next

  1. We read the specification

    If anything material is missing — grade, rating, area classification — we come back with questions rather than guessing.

  2. We identify the sourcing route

    Suppliers assessed for that product, with lead time and the documentation the item will carry.

  3. You get a delivered price

    Priced to the delivery point you asked for, including freight and clearance, so it can be compared with other offers directly.
